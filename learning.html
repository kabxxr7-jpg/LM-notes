Year-round soft maxxing

Soft maxxing is non-invasive work: skin, hair, fat, posture, colour. It compounds if you do it all year.

This is the default. You do not wait for surgery to start looking better.

Switching focus to hardmaxxing

Hard maxxing is invasive: it changes bone and structure. It is later, after basics and soft work are locked.

Surgery on a neglected face still looks like a neglected face with new bone.

how much specifc harmony affect post surgerical appearence

How well all facial features work together as a whole, rather than how good any single feature looks.

Strong harmony creates a cohesive, balanced appearance with minimal visual distraction.

the anatomy of the face

The underlying bone structure and soft-tissue features form the foundation of facial aesthetics

Structure around the cheekbones, brow, jaw, chin, and midface strongly influences overall appearance.

the science of facial ratios

Feature spacing, facial thirds, width-to-height ratios, and overall proportion are key considerations.

colouring and the contrast of the face

Skin clarity, hair colour, eyebrows, and facial contrast influence how defined and visually striking features appear. Good colouring can enhance contrast and make the face look more cohesive and vibrant.

the importance of the eye area

The eyes are a major focal point and heavily influence expression, attractiveness, and perceived facial harmony. Shape, spacing, symmetry, eyelid exposure, brows, and the surrounding orbital area all contribute

the importance of the lower third

The jaw, chin, lips, and surrounding soft tissue create the structure of the lower face. A well-proportioned lower third provides definition and helps balance the face with the midface and upper third.
