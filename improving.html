lock in the maintainence

Maintaining good grooming, skin quality, fitness, posture, and overall presentation preserves your strongest features. Consistency matters because a strong baseline is more valuable than constantly chasing new changes

target the leftover weak spots

Identify the features that are noticeably holding back overall harmony rather than trying to change everything. Targeting a few genuine weak points can create a more noticeable improvement while preserving your strengths.

switch to hardmaxxing once basics and softmaxxing is covered

Permanent or significant interventions aimed at changing underlying features, such as orthodontics, surgery, or other procedures. They should be considered carefully because the risks, costs, and potential outcomes are much greater than grooming-based improvements

eyelashes are very important for the eye area

Eyelash length, density, and curl can enhance eye definition and make the eyes appear more prominent. Well-framed eyes can improve perceived attractiveness without altering the underlying eye structure.

a muscular neck is one of the highes roi muscle groups to train for looks

Neck thickness, definition, and posture influence the transition between the jaw and shoulders. A proportionate, well-positioned neck can make the jawline appear stronger and improve overall facial-to-body harmony
