COLOURING

Skin tone, hair colour, eyebrows, and overall contrast strongly influence facial harmony and visual appeal.

Good colouring can make features look sharper and create a more cohesive appearance.

EYES

The eyes are one of the biggest focal points of the face and strongly affect attractiveness and expression.

Eye shape, spacing, symmetry, and the surrounding brow area contribute heavily to perceived facial harmony.

JAW&CHIN

A defined jaw and proportionate chin provide structure to the lower third of the face.

Their size, projection, and relationship with the rest of the face can significantly affect facial definition and balance.

FACIAL RATIOS

Balanced proportions between the upper, middle, and lower thirds generally make a face appear more harmonious.

Ratios such as facial width-to-height and feature spacing help determine overall symmetry and aesthetic balance.
